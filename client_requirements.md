# L’ESPRIT VOYAGES — Client Content & Asset Requirements Checklist

This document outlines the exact content copy, media assets, licensing credentials, and technical configurations needed from the website owner (**L’Esprit Voyages**) to complete the final deployment of the luxury website.

---

## 1. 📂 Brand & Legal Credentials
To build trust and satisfy tourism regulations in Sri Lanka and the Maldives, we require:
- [ ] **Official Registrations:**
  - Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority (SLTDA) license number.
  - Maldives Ministry of Tourism registration details.
- [ ] **Physical Address & Contact Info:**
  - Official headquarters address.
  - Primary contact phone number (including country codes for luxury international clientele).
  - Dedicated concierge booking email address (e.g., `reservations@lespritvoyages.com`).
- [ ] **Legal Documents:**
  - Terms of Service copy (specific to bespoke crafting & luxury deposits).
  - Privacy & GDPR Compliance copy (critical for high-net-worth European/US clients).

Luxury UI/UX relies heavily on immersive, high-end visuals. Please supply:
- [ ] **Branding:**
  - Official vector logo (SVG format preferred) in both Dark and Gold/White variants.

- [ ] **Cinematic Video Backgrounds:**
  - 10-15 second looping horizontal drone footage (MP4 / WebM format, < 5MB optimized) showcasing:
    - Sri Lankan mist-covered tea gardens.

- [ ] **Premium Vehicle Fleet Images:**
  - Authentic, high-quality images of the luxury fleet vehicles (e.g., Mercedes S-Class, Land Cruiser Prado, Luxury Touring Coaches) on transparent or elegant backgrounds.
- [ ] **Ambient Audio Soundscapes:**
  - 1-2 minute looping, royalty-free audio tracks (MP3/OGG) representing Sri Lankan and Maldivian nature sounds (subtle birds, ocean waves, gentle wind) for the immersive audio player.
